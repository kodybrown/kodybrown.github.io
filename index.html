Software architect, inventor, and lifelong tinkerer.

I'm a husband and father based in Utah. I enjoy making things, figuring out how they work, and finding simpler ways to solve problems.

Away from software, I enjoy games with my wife and kids, playing guitar, working on my 1989 Land Cruiser, and tinkering with amateur radio and electronics.

My faith is part of my life. I'm a member of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.
