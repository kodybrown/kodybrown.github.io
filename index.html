Kody Brown

Software architect, inventor, and lifelong tinkerer.

I'm a husband and father based in Utah. I enjoy making things, figuring out how they work, and finding simpler ways to solve problems.

A little about me

Away from software, I enjoy games with my wife and kids, playing guitar, working on my 1989 Land Cruiser FJ62, 1967 Ford Galaxie, Schoolie, Gokarts, and tinkering with amateur radio, Meshtastic and electronics.

My faith is an important part of my life. I'm a member of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.

And if you were looking for the other Kody Brown: sorry, that's not me. 😉

Professional background

My work spans software architecture, developer tools, and embedded/IoT systems. I co-founded Vutility and helped create HotDrop, a wireless, battery-less energy sensor. I've also been a technical reviewer for multiple editions of Essential C#.
